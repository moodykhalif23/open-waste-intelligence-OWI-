Waste operations, run on evidence

Know which bins need collecting today.

OpenWaste is a phone app for collectors and an operations dashboard for the people who plan the day. Photos and collector-entered fill levels inform material estimates, collection priorities, routes, and operational reports.

See what you get How it works

From photo to decision, in four steps.

Snap

A collector captures a photo and a quick fill-level tap. Reports can queue without a connection and sync later.

Understand

A model estimates material from the photo; the collector enters fill level. Detected people are blurred before primary image storage.

Decide

Bin health ranks what needs collecting today, and the route is built around it.

Share

Delayed aggregates — never raw photos or exact locations — are available through a public data API for counties, researchers, and residents.

Collection runs on guesswork.

Trucks sweep fixed routes whether bins are full or empty. Overflow is discovered by complaint, days late. Recyclable value leaves the yard unweighed, and the crews doing the hardest work have the least data to show for it.

None of that is a sensor problem. A collector already stands in front of every bin, every day, holding a phone. That moment is the measurement — it has simply never been captured.

Routes that ignore reality

Fixed schedules send vehicles to empty bins and miss the ones already overflowing.

Overflow found by complaint

The first signal is a resident calling, long after the pile started building.

Value that leaks away

Recyclables leave unpriced, so nobody can prove what a day of work was worth.

For operations teams

Rank, plan, allocate, measure.

Hard lines, written into the code.

  • Images are screened to blur detected people, not to identify them.
  • Detected people are blurred before the photo is stored. The unblurred original is quarantined and purged automatically within 72 hours of upload.
  • A collector can delete any of their recently sent photos from the phone. The image is erased from storage, no questions asked.
  • The public feed contains delayed aggregates only; thin cells are withheld, and photos and exact coordinates are not published.
  • Classifier reports include per-class results on a held-out public dataset. Local field accuracy still needs validation; reviewer corrections can be exported for model work.
  • Sign-ins and other security-relevant actions are kept in an append-only audit trail, enforced by the database.

Your data. Your hardware. No lock-in.

Run OpenWaste on hardware you control — a laptop, a mini-PC in an office, or a small server. There is no per-seat charge, and you retain direct access to your operational data. Source code is available on GitHub under the Apache-2.0 license.
